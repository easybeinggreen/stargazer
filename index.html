Stargazer

Point the phone at the sky (or a window, or the floor) and get back a labelled view of the stars, planets and Moon that are there. Feasibility spikes, cheapest-risk first.

Phase 1 — Sensor spike. Can the A35's browser give a usable compass heading? Rate, jitter, drift, indoors vs out.

Phase 2 (astronomy pipeline) and Phase 3 (three.js sky diorama) land here as they're built.
