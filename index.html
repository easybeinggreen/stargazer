Stargazer

Point the phone at the sky (or a window, or the floor) and get back a labelled view of the stars, planets and Moon that are there. Feasibility spikes, cheapest-risk first.

Phase 1 — Sensor spike. Can the A35's browser give a usable compass heading? Rate, jitter, drift, indoors vs out. Phase 2 — Astronomy pipeline. alt/az for Sun, Moon, planets & bright stars, computed locally. Cross-check against Stellarium Web. Phase 3 — Sky diorama. three.js planetarium: 5,000-star catalogue, constellation lines, planets, drag to look around, phone-orientation mode, camera pass-through. The go/no-go gate.

Next: capture → shareable labelled image.
